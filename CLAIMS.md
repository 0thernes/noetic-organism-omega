Epistemic tier definitions:

- `empirical`: Measured over locked Philox4x32-10 seeds (`N=48`: `5001..5024` + `5101..5124`) with exact DP Wilcoxon & Holm-Bonferroni FWER.
- `indicator-only`: Architectural/behavioral proxy per ADR 0018 Rule-4 & Rule-6; **never** a claim of phenomenal consciousness or sentience.
- `open-conjecture`: Open problem referenced for context only (never claimed solved).
