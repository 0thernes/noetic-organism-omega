# Limitations, Threats to Validity & Real-vs-Metaphor Boundary (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

`noetic-organism-omega v2.2.0` enforces strict epistemic hygiene. Every limitation and boundary below is an explicit constraint on how results may be interpreted.

## 1. Real-vs-Metaphor Boundary (CRITICAL)

- **Zero Consciousness or Sentience Claims:** All 14 Butlin indicator checks (`L1_BUTLIN14`), all 10 consciousness-framework checks (`L2_FRAMEWORKS10`), and 5 mirrored CQM items (`CQM13, CQM19..22`) are strictly **`EXECUTED_PASS_INDICATOR_ONLY`** computational/architectural proxies. Passing these structural lesion tests demonstrates that specific C11 state equations causally drive specific behavioral readouts inside a 6,776-byte simulation struct—**nothing more**. They provide **zero** evidence of subjective qualia, phenomenal experience, sentience, or moral patienthood.
- **Biological Metaphors Are Compact Toy Equations:** Terms such as *sLTC cortex*, *apical dCaAP XOR binding*, *global workspace ignition*, *REM reality monitoring*, *XPBD hydrostatic coelom*, *Gánti Chemoton*, *Blackiston metamorphosis*, and *autopoietic tissue repair* denote deterministic low-dimensional numerical models (16-unit cortex, 16 patches, 4 peers), not wet-lab neurobiology, real cellular biochemistry, or insect neuroanatomy.
- **Mega-Atlas Non-Retirement:** **`0 / 1,900`** overarching problems in `0thernes/unsolved-problems-mega-atlas` are solved or retired (`docs/MEGA_ATLAS_CROSSCHECK.md`).

## 2. Formal-to-C11 Verification Boundary

- **Not CompCert / VST Verified:** The 68 Lean 4.35.0 theorems (`lean/NoeticProofs.lean` + `lean/AdversarialTests.lean`) formally verify discrete mathematical specifications (`Ledger10`, 11-slot `Ledger11`, `ring0ApplyEdit`, `packB158`, `sum1To`, bitmask/memory bounds, and 8 adversarial false-variant refutations) plus concrete integer/boolean constants exported by a live C11 run (`NoeticProofs/LiveC11Receipt.lean`, audited by `N24`, `N32`, `N48`, `N59`). Lean does **not** verify the C11 source files (`src/*.c`) or Clang/GCC machine-code emission.
- **Unbounded `Int` vs. C11 `int64_t`:** Both `Ledger10` (10 fields) and `Ledger11` (all 11 slots `p0..p10` plus `initialTotal`, closing the `v2.1.0` 10-vs-11 slot gap) use arbitrary-precision `Int`, whereas C11 uses signed 64-bit integers (`int64_t`). Overflow avoidance in C11 relies on bounded initial pool magnitudes ($\sim 10^7~\text{uq} \ll 9.2 \times 10^{18}$) and runtime validation rather than a Lean `Int64` overflow proof.
- **Exact Breakdown of the 68 Theorems:**
  - **46** parametric model, `Ledger11`, encoding, strict `1.6x` ratio (`N60`), non-vacuity witness, and adversarial false-variant refutation theorems (`A01..A08`).
  - **18** closed numeral identities with no free variables (`N12`, `N13`, `N16`, `N23`, `N27`, `N31`, `N34`, `N38..N47`, `N52`; for example `N41`: `825 - 5 = 820`). They are explicitly labeled in code and paper as bookkeeping arithmetic that certifies nothing about the engine.
  - **4** live C11 receipt constant checks (`N24`, `N32`, `N48`, `N59`) that `decide`-check exported constants from one run (seed 5001).
- **Abstract Models vs. Physics / Hardware:** `N02`, `N06`, and `N57` prove non-decrease of the integer heat field `p8` under guarded transfers (not physical thermodynamics); `N25`, `N50`, `N51`, and `A03` prove the abstract `if region < 4` guard identity (not hardware memory protection). No Lean theorem covers the WSE spectral norm `||W||_2 <= 0.99` (tested empirically in `GATE-09`).
- **Cross-OS Determinism Scope (`v2.2.0` Resolution of E-10):** In `v2.1.0`, 89 system `libm` calls and missing `-ffp-contract=off` caused world-state SHA-256 divergence between Windows and Linux. `v2.2.0` eliminates those calls via 8 pure IEEE-754 portable functions (`noetic_{exp,log,log2,tanh,sin,cos,atan2,hypot}_det`, `max_rel_err = 2.274e-16` in `HW15`) and enforces `-ffp-contract=off` + binary LF receipt export + CI `diff -u`, achieving 0-bit identical SHA-256 across x86_64 Win64 Clang and Linux GCC. These IEEE-754 approximations are empirically audited in `HW15`, not formally proved in Lean/Flocq, and non-IEEE-754 architectures or compilers that ignore `-ffp-contract=off` are outside the tested parity envelope.

## 3. Statistical & Experimental Limitations

- **Deterministic Low-Variance $d_z$ Inflation:** In deterministic mechanistic suites (`L8_SOFT40`, `L4_FREAK100`, `L5_LIFE500`), paired intact-vs-knockout differences across locked seeds frequently exhibit near-zero variance, producing very large Cohen's $d_z$ values ($> 50$ or capped at `50.00`). These values indicate clean deterministic separation under controlled software perturbations and must not be compared naively to noisy wet-lab biological effect sizes.
- **Honest Classical Parity Ties:** Five quantum-inspired checks (`F-092`, `N01`, `N02`, `N50`, `LIFE-L040-A5`) show exact `0.00` lift over matched-parameter classical controls (`p_Holm = 1.00`). No quantum computational advantage is claimed anywhere in this repository.
- **Single-Host Windows Multi-Kernel Constraint:** Locally on Windows, `lean4lean.exe` cannot parse Windows `.olean` path headers and `lake check` / `lake comparator` require Linux `bubblewrap` (`bwrap`). Full 6/6 kernel execution (adding `lean4lean`, `lake comparator --paranoid`, and `scripts/test_lean_negative.py --comparator`) runs on `ubuntu-latest` in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
- **Single-Author / AI-Assisted Provenance & Peer Review:** This is an independent, AI-assisted research artifact and has **not** undergone external peer review or third-party independent replication yet (`REPLICATION.md`).
