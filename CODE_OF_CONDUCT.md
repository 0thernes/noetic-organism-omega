# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the `noetic-organism-omega` research repository a harassment-free, constructive, and scientifically rigorous experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or affiliation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive technical and scientific environment:
- Rigorous, evidence-based critique of code, mathematical formalizations, statistical protocols, and epistemic claims.
- Transparent disclosure of AI tool usage, assumptions, negative controls, and reproducibility findings.
- Respectful, direct, and constructive communication in issues and discussions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, or discrimination.
- Fabricating benchmark numbers, hiding negative controls, or submitting unverified AI-generated slop without running the verification suite.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to Alexander Stephen Donahue at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Adapted from Contributor Covenant v2.1 (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>).
